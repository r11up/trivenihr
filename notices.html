Know the rules

Foreign employmentis regulated

Recruitment out of Nepal is governed by the Foreign Employment Act, 2064 (2007) and the Foreign Employment Regulation, together with directives issued by the Government of Nepal from time to time. Both employers and workers are protected by those rules — and both are bound by them.

We publish this page so that anyone dealing with us can check our position independently rather than taking our word for it.

  • Only a licensed agency may recruit workers for foreign employment
  • Every demand must be approved by the Department of Foreign Employment before recruitment begins
  • Every worker must hold a labour permit before departure
  • Service charges to workers are capped by law and must be receipted
  • No recruitment may be conducted through an unlicensed agent
