Demand submission checklist

Everything needed to start recruitment, attested by the Nepalese diplomatic mission accredited to your country:

  • Demand letter
  • Power of attorney in favour of Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd.
  • Employment agreement / contract
  • Guarantee letter
  • Copy of trade licence or commercial registration
  • Visa authority or block visa approval, where applicable
