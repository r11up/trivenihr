Who we are

We link Nepalese workerswith global employers

Opening doors to international opportunities.

In response to the soaring demand for Nepalese workforce in countries such as Qatar, the U.A.E., K.S.A., Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Malaysia, a dedicated team of professionals came together to establish Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd. Our commitment to delivering timely and flawless recruitment solutions with unwavering ethics has been our guiding principle. Drawing from our early recruitment experiences, we have transformed into a success story within the industry, earning a reputable name in the market.

The professionals at Triveni HR have been meticulously chosen, possessing the knowledge and skills essential for the recruitment process. We have technically qualified recruitment managers who match candidates to job specifications precisely, saving our clients valuable time and resources. Our team members are highly qualified and possess vast experience in projects and HR, and we prioritise their professional development through training — reinforcing the strength of our organisation.

Our investment in staff development, including flexible working schedules and innovative HR initiatives, has allowed us to deploy a significant workforce in the countries above in recent years. We are eager to expand our reach to more overseas destinations that align with the needs of Nepalese employees. We humbly request the opportunity to serve you with our esteemed services.

Our house

Built forthe work

Recruitment is a paperwork business as much as a people business. Where you sit matters.

  • A core team of more than 50 people working from our own four-floor building in Samakhushi — three floors of offices and three flats.
  • Our own kitchen. Breakfast and lunch are cooked in-house for staff every working day.
  • In the heart of the capital. Next to the Nepal Police Cyber Bureau, the Nepal Police Dog Training centre and the Nepal Police Music Association, close to the Nepal Police armed facility.
  • Everything within reach. Ten minutes from the city centre, with the Department of Passports and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs a short ride away — which is exactly why paperwork moves quickly here.

Welcome message

At Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd., we extend a warm and sincere welcome as you embark on this exploration of our dedicated workforce solutions. With a steadfast commitment to excellence that spans over 23 years, we have been your trusted partner since our establishment in 2002. As you journey through these pages, we invite you to discover the essence of our unwavering dedication to service, integrity and transparency.

Our paramount goal is to simplify your recruitment journey, making it as seamless and rewarding as possible. We understand that navigating the intricate world of recruitment can be both exciting and challenging. Here at Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd., we aspire to be your guiding light, ensuring that your career aspirations harmoniously align with the boundless opportunities we offer. As we open our doors to you, we wholeheartedly embrace the chance to help you achieve your professional goals and foster a future where excellence knows no bounds.

Our Vision

Our vision is to lead the way in international recruitment and career management services across all industries. To achieve this, we invest in our people, foster partnerships with our clients, and employ a unique recruitment methodology that sets us apart in the industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to understand the employer’s needs and provide the best qualified candidate. We are a professional organisation that communicates with clarity, honesty, integrity and respect. To remain an industry leader, we must continue to deliver the best solutions that will exceed expectations, because we are recruiting an employer’s most important assets: their workers. We know that ultimately we will be judged not only on our firm’s success, but also on the partnership successfully developed between us and our clients.

Tulsi Paudel

Founder, Chairman & Group CEO

Head Office, Kathmandu

Tulsi Paudel founded Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd. in 2002 and has led it ever since. He steered the company through its formative years as Managing Director and, for the past four years, has served as Chairman and Group CEO. He is the owner of the company.

His remit covers the whole Triveni group — the recruitment business alongside the sister companies in travel, health care and training — and he sets the standard the company is known for: place the right worker, do it lawfully, and stand behind the placement afterwards.

Outside the business he is active in community service, supporting Rotary programmes in Kathmandu and the company’s education work in rural government schools.

Padam Chhetri

Operations Director

Head Office, Kathmandu

Padam Chhetri runs day-to-day operations from the head office in Kathmandu.

His team handles the parts of a placement that decide whether it succeeds: demand letter attestation, Department of Foreign Employment approvals, medical scheduling, visa processing, labour permits and departure logistics. He is the person who keeps a file moving from approval to boarding pass.

For employers, he is the straight answer to “where exactly are we?” on any active demand.

Rajan Lamichhane

Recruitment Director

Head Office, Kathmandu

Rajan Lamichhane leads sourcing and selection across our five offices.

He turns a client’s specification into a shortlist that holds up — advertising, screening, trade testing and interview coordination — and he is deliberate about not sending candidates who only look right on paper.

His knowledge of Nepal’s regional labour markets is why we recruit from the eastern hills, the Terai and the western districts rather than relying on Kathmandu alone.
