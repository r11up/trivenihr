Tulsi Paudel

Founder, Chairman & Group CEO

Head Office, Kathmandu

Tulsi Paudel founded Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd. in 2002 and has led it ever since. He steered the company through its formative years as Managing Director and, for the past four years, has served as Chairman and Group CEO. He is the owner of the company.

His remit covers the whole Triveni group — the recruitment business alongside the sister companies in travel, health care and training — and he sets the standard the company is known for: place the right worker, do it lawfully, and stand behind the placement afterwards.

Outside the business he is active in community service, supporting Rotary programmes in Kathmandu and the company’s education work in rural government schools.

Padam Chhetri

Operations Director

Head Office, Kathmandu

Padam Chhetri runs day-to-day operations from the head office in Kathmandu.

His team handles the parts of a placement that decide whether it succeeds: demand letter attestation, Department of Foreign Employment approvals, medical scheduling, visa processing, labour permits and departure logistics. He is the person who keeps a file moving from approval to boarding pass.

For employers, he is the straight answer to “where exactly are we?” on any active demand.

Rajan Lamichhane

Recruitment Director

Head Office, Kathmandu

Rajan Lamichhane leads sourcing and selection across our five offices.

He turns a client’s specification into a shortlist that holds up — advertising, screening, trade testing and interview coordination — and he is deliberate about not sending candidates who only look right on paper.

His knowledge of Nepal’s regional labour markets is why we recruit from the eastern hills, the Terai and the western districts rather than relying on Kathmandu alone.
