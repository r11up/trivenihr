For candidates

How toapply

  1. Check the vacancy

    Read the announcement on our Facebook page or call the office to confirm the post, destination country and interview date.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Passport valid for at least six months, passport-size photographs, citizenship certificate, and any trade certificates or experience letters relevant to the post. Need help building a CV? Message our reception.

  3. Attend the interview

    Come to the announced venue on time. Some posts require a practical trade test as well as an interview.

  4. Medical and documentation

    If selected, complete the medical examination at an approved centre. We then process your visa and labour permit.

  5. Orientation and departure

    Complete the mandatory pre-departure orientation training, collect your documents and travel.
