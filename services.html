Step by step

The recruitmentprocess

Every placement we make follows the procedure set out in Nepal’s Foreign Employment Act, 2064 (2007) and its regulations. Nothing is done through an unlicensed agent.

  1. Demand submission

    You send us the demand letter, power of attorney and employment agreement, attested by the Nepalese diplomatic mission responsible for your country.

  2. Government approval

    We file the documents with the Department of Foreign Employment and obtain prior approval to recruit for your vacancies.

  3. Advertising & sourcing

    The vacancy is advertised as required by law and circulated through our four offices and our network of local agents.

  4. Screening & shortlisting

    Applications are checked against your specification — trade, experience, age, language and documentation — before anyone reaches you.

  5. Interview & trade test

    You interview in person, by video, or delegate selection to us. Practical trade tests are arranged for technical roles.

  6. Medical examination

    Selected candidates are examined at a GAMCA/approved medical centre, including through our sister company, Diamond Health Care Center.

  7. Visa & labour permit

    We handle visa stamping at the relevant embassy and obtain the labour permit and final clearance from the Department of Foreign Employment.

  8. Orientation & departure

    Workers complete the mandatory pre-departure orientation, then we arrange ticketing and confirm flight details with you.

  9. Post-placement follow-up

    We stay in contact after arrival so that any issue with documentation, accommodation or the role itself can be resolved quickly.

Before we start

What we needfrom you

To begin recruitment for your company, Nepalese law requires the following documents, attested by the Nepalese Embassy or diplomatic mission accredited to your country.

  • Demand letter — stating job titles, number of workers, salary, benefits, contract period and working hours
  • Power of attorney — authorising Triveni HR Solution Pvt. Ltd. to recruit on your behalf
  • Employment agreement — the contract to be signed by each selected worker
  • Guarantee letter and a copy of your trade licence or commercial registration
  • Visa authority or block visa approval, where the destination country requires it

Not sure about the wording? Message our reception on WhatsApp and we will send you a sample demand letter and agreement to adapt.

Common questions

How long does the whole process take?

It depends on the destination country, the number of workers and how quickly government approvals come through. As a general guide, straightforward bulk recruitment runs from about six to ten weeks between approved demand and departure. We will give you a realistic schedule for your specific case before we start.

Can you conduct the selection for us?

Yes. Many employers delegate first-round selection to us and approve the final shortlist remotely. We can also arrange video interviews, or host your delegation in Kathmandu for in-person selection and trade tests.

Do you recruit for small orders as well as bulk?

We do. Whether you need a single technician or several hundred workers, the compliance steps are the same and we apply the same process.

What about visa quota restrictions?

Where quota is a constraint, we can discuss deploying workers under our own sponsorship arrangements and managing them on your behalf. Talk to us about what is possible in your jurisdiction.

Are you licensed to send workers to my country?

Our licence covers foreign employment recruitment under the Foreign Employment Act. Approvals are granted per destination country by the Department of Foreign Employment. Ask us and we will confirm the current position for your country before you commit to anything.
